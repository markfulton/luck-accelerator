# Where to look

Use the app's web search to discover, then read the actual page to confirm. If your search tool ignores `site:`, search the platform name with the topic instead (`meetup strength training manchester`). These are starting points, not the only places. A good local find often comes from a source that is not on this list: a city business journal, a council's business support page, a university's entrepreneurship centre.

## A. High value local connections

Look for people and businesses in their public business role. Good kinds:

- **Referral partners:** businesses that serve the same customer before or after the user does. A wedding photographer and a florist. A web designer and a bookkeeper. A fitness coach and a physiotherapy clinic.
- **Connectors:** people whose job is meeting people. Event organizers, chamber of commerce staff, coworking community managers, local podcast hosts, local newsletter writers, business school and incubator staff, BNI chapter leaders.
- **Likely customers:** businesses that match the user's ideal customer and show a trigger, such as a recent opening, a new location, a rebrand, hiring, a funding announcement, an award.

Where to search:

| Source | How |
|---|---|
| Local business news | `"<city>" new business opening <month year>`, the city's business journal, local news "new in town" columns |
| Business directories | Google Maps and Yelp listings to discover, then the business's own site to confirm |
| Chambers and business associations | `<city> chamber of commerce members directory`, local business improvement districts, trade associations |
| Coworking spaces | `coworking <city>`, then the space's events and community pages |
| Local podcasts and newsletters | `<city> business podcast`, `<city> small business newsletter` |
| Award and "best of" lists | `<city> best small businesses <year>`, `40 under 40 <city>` |
| Incubators and accelerators | `<city> startup incubator`, university enterprise centres |

What to record for each: the name, the business, why they fit, the public page you confirmed them on, and the public contact route shown there (contact form, business email, booking page, or "meet at event X"). Never a personal profile or personal contact detail.

## B. Events

| Source | Notes |
|---|---|
| Meetup | `site:meetup.com <city> <topic>`. Read the event page for the date. Meetup often refuses scripts, so confirm it in a browser. A group page can show dates an event page hides |
| Eventbrite | `site:eventbrite.com <city> <topic>` or the country domain (eventbrite.co.uk, eventbrite.com.au). Check the date and the ticket price |
| Luma | `site:lu.ma <city> <topic>` and `site:luma.com <city>`. Strong for tech, startup and creator events |
| Chambers of commerce | The chamber's own events calendar. Many networking breakfasts are open to non members |
| Coworking spaces | Their events page. Often free and open to the public |
| BNI and other referral groups | Chapter pages list visitor days |
| Conferences and trade shows | `<niche> conference <year> <country>`. Include only if inside 60 days, or flag as "book ahead" |
| Libraries, councils, universities | Free business workshops. UK: local growth hubs and council business support. US: SBA, SCORE and SBDC workshops |
| Online events | Luma, Eventbrite "online", community calendars inside public Skool or Circle pages |

Keep events dated in the next 30 days, plus standout events up to 60 days out tagged `book-ahead`. The date must show on the page, or follow from a stated pattern for a recurring event. If a calendar will not load, look for the same event on Eventbrite, Meetup or Luma before dropping it. Record date, start time with time zone if shown, venue or "online", price, and the registration link.

## C. Online networks and communities

| Source | How to find | How to confirm |
|---|---|---|
| Skool | `site:skool.com <niche>`, skool.com/discovery | The community's about page shows member count, price, and whether it is free. Note if it is paid |
| Subreddits | `site:reddit.com/r <niche>`, `<niche> subreddit` | Reddit often refuses scripts. Confirm the subreddit exists and is active by opening it in a browser. Read its rules in the sidebar before drafting a post. Many subreddits ban self promotion entirely; say so |
| Discord servers | disboard.org tags, `<niche> discord server` | A public invite page or a listing with a recent bump date. Invite links expire; prefer the server's own website link |
| Slack communities | `<niche> slack community` | The community's own site with a join form |
| Facebook groups | `<niche> facebook group <city>` | Only include if a public page shows the group name and member count. Never join for the user |
| Forums and niche sites | `<niche> forum`, Indie Hackers groups, trade association member forums | A recent post date |
| Newsletters | `<niche> newsletter` | A recent issue date |
| LinkedIn groups and pages | Search only | LinkedIn refuses scripts and hides most pages behind a login. Include only when a public search result confirms it, and never automate anything on LinkedIn |

## D. Sub niches

A sub niche is a slice of the user's market that is narrow enough to own: a type of customer, a situation, or a place. Evidence that one is worth it:

- a community or subreddit exists for exactly that slice and is active;
- events exist for that slice;
- people in those communities ask for help with the problem the user solves;
- few providers speak to that slice directly.

For each sub niche, name it in a few words, give two pieces of evidence with links, and say in one line how the user's offer would be described to that slice.

## E. Groups and Skool communities to join

This section is a short ranked list drawn from B and C plus local clubs: the places where joining and showing up every week pays off. A free, active, local or tightly niche group beats a huge general one. Say whether each is free or paid, and how often it meets or posts.

## Country notes

- **United States:** chambers, BNI, SCORE and SBDC workshops, Meetup, Eventbrite, Luma, coworking spaces, city business journals.
- **United Kingdom:** chambers of commerce, Federation of Small Businesses (FSB) events, local growth hubs, council business support, Eventbrite UK, Meetup, 4Networking and other breakfast networks.
- **Canada, Australia, New Zealand:** chambers, regional business centres, Eventbrite on the country domain, Meetup.
- **Elsewhere:** search in the local language as well as English, and look for the national small business agency's events page.
